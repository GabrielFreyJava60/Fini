# Fini Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

Fini ("the app") is a personal budgeting app developed by Gabriel Freidlin ("we").

## Summary
**Fini does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.** Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores
Transactions, budgets, categories, settings and forecasts you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. Encryption keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. We have no servers and no access to this data.

## Network
The app works fully offline and does not send your financial data over the network. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

## Backups and export
If you create an encrypted backup or export, the file is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your 24-word recovery phrase, and you choose where to save it (for example, the Files app or iCloud Drive). That storage is governed by its provider's privacy policy (e.g., Apple). Device backups made by iOS (iCloud Backup / Finder) may include app data under Apple's terms.

## Recovery phrase
Your recovery phrase is shown only to you. We cannot recover it or your data if it is lost.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Delete the app to remove all its data from your device. Remove any backup files you exported yourself.

## Changes
We will post changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
