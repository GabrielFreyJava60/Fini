## English (en-US) — primary
- **Name:** Fini: Budget & Cash Flow
- **Subtitle:** Private budget & forecast
- **Promotional text:** Know where your money is heading. Fini forecasts your balance to next payday, flags spending stress early, and keeps every number on your iPhone.
- **Keywords:** budget,expense,tracker,cash flow,forecast,spending,money,savings,installments,receipt,shekel
- **Description:**

Fini is a private budget and cash-flow app that lives entirely on your iPhone. No bank logins, no accounts, no servers — your data never leaves the device.

RUNWAY FORECAST
See the likely range of your balance until next payday and for the next 90 days, built from your own recurring income and spending.

PULSE STRESS INDEX
A 0–100 score that shows how fast you are spending against your pay cycle, with a plain-language explanation of what drives it.

FAST LOGGING
Add an expense in a couple of taps. Credit-card billing dates and installment purchases (tashlumim) are scheduled automatically and split to the agora.

BUDGETS & CATEGORIES
Ready-made categories in English, Hebrew and Russian. Set monthly budgets and see what's left at a glance.

OFFLINE ASSISTANT
Ask "How much can I spend until payday?" or "Why is my stress score high?" in English, Hebrew or Russian. Answers are computed on the device; nothing is sent anywhere.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• No sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Encrypted backups protected by a 24-word recovery phrase
• Encryption keys stored in the iOS Keychain

Fini is a personal budgeting tool and does not provide financial, tax or investment advice.
